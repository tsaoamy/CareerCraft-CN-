# CareerCraft-CN-
在调研了大量海外产品后，我发现很多 AI 简历工具虽然能够快速生成内容，却很难真正理解一个人的成长经历。每次求职都需要重新修改简历、重新整理项目经历，投入大量重复劳动。而对于学生和职场新人来说，真正有价值的往往不是简历本身，而是这些年积累下来的项目、竞赛、实习与成长故事。  因此，我尝试从“职业资产管理”的角度重新思考这个问题：如果用户只需要记录一次自己的经历，AI 就能够根据不同岗位自动分析需求、匹配能力、生成专属简历，并进一步提供面试辅导和职业规划建议，那么求职这件事是否会变得更加高效和个性化？
After researching a large number of overseas products, I found that although many AI resume tools can quickly generate content, they have difficulty truly understanding a person's growth experience. Every time one applies for a job, the resume needs to be revised and project experiences reorganized, involving a lot of repetitive work. For students and young professionals, the real value often lies not in the resume itself, but in the projects, competitions, internships, and growth stories accumulated over the years. Therefore, I tried to rethink this issue from the perspective of 'career asset management': if users only need to record their experiences once, and AI can automatically analyze job requirements, match skills, generate a personalized resume, and further provide interview coaching and career planning advice, would the job application process become more efficient and personalized?
